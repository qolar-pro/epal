Questions.

Do I need an API key?

No. DonutSMP switched off its public API, so GoNuts reads the market from what the game shows: order house fills, live auction prices and your own sales.

Does it cost anything?

No. GoNuts is free and open source under the MIT licence.

How do I stop it?

Press the End key and it stops at once. You can also stop it from the buttons on the Discord panel.

Where does it read my balance?

From the sidebar scoreboard. Turn it on in /settings after you join DonutSMP.

Is it safe to use on DonutSMP?

Watching prices and reading the screens carry no ban risk. Live trading does: DonutSMP's rules ban scripts, macros and auto-clickers, and you choose to take that risk.

What do I need to run it?

Minecraft Java 26.2, Fabric Loader for 26.2, the Fabric API mod and Java 25.

Is it affiliated with DonutSMP?

No. GoNuts is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by DonutSMP.

Know the risk before you turn it on.

DonutSMP's rules ban scripts, macros and auto-clickers, and enforcement can reach every account linked to a player. Running live trading is a ban risk that you, the account owner, choose to take.

Watching prices and reading the screens carry no such risk; live trading does. GoNuts is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by DonutSMP.
